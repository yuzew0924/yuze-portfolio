About Me

Hi, I’m Ethan, a Computer Science student at the University of Washington interested in building intelligent systems that can reason, make decisions, and interact with the real world.

My work spans machine learning, NLP, agentic AI, and full-stack software development. I enjoy turning ideas into end-to-end systems, from designing algorithms and backend services to evaluating how AI systems behave under uncertainty.

I’m currently expanding my interests toward robotics, computer vision, and autonomous systems, particularly at the intersection of AI reasoning and real-world decision-making.
