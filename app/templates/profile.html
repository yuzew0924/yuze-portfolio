About Me

About Me

Hi! I'm Yuze Wang, a 12th-grade student at Beijing No.80 High School with a passion for programming and photography. I specialize in travel photography, creating images that tell stories.

My Journey

My Journey

My work has been showcased at school events, where I shared my perspective on the world through my lens. Photography for me is about preserving fleeting moments and finding beauty in the unexpected. Over the years, I've traveled to places like Beijing, Inner Mongolia, Shanghai, Shandong, Henan, Hong Kong, and Macau (China), Vancouver (Canada), the Maldives, Phuket and Bangkok (Thailand), as well as Osaka, Kyoto, and Kobe (Japan), capturing unique moments along the way.

What I Love

What I Love

I love capturing the interplay of light and shadow, especially during sunrises, sunsets, and moonlit nights. Every photo I take reflects my emotions at that moment, and each is a unique, unrepeatable expression of how I see the world.
